Bortle Scale Meter

What is Bortle scale?

▼
Level 1: Excellent Dark Sky
The Milky Way is highly detailed. Zodiacal light is visible. M33 is direct vision object.
Level 2: Typical Dark Site
The Milky Way appears complex. Some light pollution along horizons. M33 easily seen.
Level 3: Rural Sky
Some light pollution visible. The Milky Way still impressive. M33 difficult with averted vision.
Level 4: Rural/Suburban
Light pollution domes visible. Milky Way washed out but visible. M33 very difficult.
Level 5: Suburban Sky
The Milky Way is very weak or invisible near horizon. Clouds brighter than the sky.
Level 6: Bright Suburban
The Milky Way is barely visible. Sky is light gray up to 35° above horizon.
Level 7: Suburban/Urban
Entire sky is grayish. Strong light pollution domes. Milky Way invisible.
Level 8: City Sky
Sky is light gray or orange. Only bright stars and planets visible.
Level 9: Inner-City Sky
Sky is brilliantly lit. Only the Moon, planets and brightest stars visible.
